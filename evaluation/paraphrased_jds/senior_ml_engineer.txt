Senior Engineer, Applied Intelligence Platform

We are building the systems that let our products understand and generate written language
at scale. You will take models from a research notebook all the way to serving live traffic:
training them on large text corpora, evaluating answer quality offline, and running the
retrieval layer that grounds generated answers in our own documents.

What the role needs
- Several years writing production services, not just notebooks
- Depth in models that learn representations of text, and in grounding answers against a
  searchable corpus of company documents
- Comfort shipping and operating those services on managed, orchestrated infrastructure
